## THEORY

A branch of mathematics that talks about the length, volume, or area of different [geometric shapes](https://byjus.com/maths/geometric-shapes/) is called Mensuration. These shapes exist in 2 dimensions or 3 dimensions.

## Mensuration Formulas For 2D Shapes

| Shape | Area (Square units) | Perimeter (units) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Square | a<sup>2</sup> | 4a |
| Rectangle | l × b | 2 ( l + b) |
| Circle | πr<sup>2</sup> | 2 π r |
| Scalene Triangle | √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)], Where, s = (a+b+c)/2 | a+b+c |
| Isosceles Triangle | ½ × b × h | 2a + b |
| Equilateral triangle | (√3/4) × a<sup>2</sup> | 3a |
| Right Angle Triangle | ½ × b × h | b + hypotenuse + h |
| Rhombus | ½ × d1 × d2 | 4 × side |
| Parallelogram | b × h | 2(l+b) |
| Trapezium | ½ h(a+c) | a+b+c+d |

## Mensuration Formulas for 3D Shapes

| Shape | Volume (Cubic units) | Curved Surface Area (CSA) or Lateral Surface Area (LSA) (Square units) | Total Surface Area (TSA) (Square units) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cube | a<sup>3</sup> | LSA = 4 a<sup>2</sup> | 6 a<sup>2</sup> |
| Cuboid | l × b × h | LSA = 2h(l + b) | 2 (lb +bh +hl) |
| Sphere | (4/3) π r<sup>3</sup> | 4 π r<sup>2</sup> | 4 π r<sup>2</sup> |
| Hemisphere | (⅔) π r<sup>3</sup> | 2 π r <sup>2</sup> | 3 π r <sup>2</sup> |
| Cylinder | π r <sup>2</sup> h | 2π r h | 2πrh + 2πr<sup>2</sup> |
| Cone | (⅓) π r<sup>2</sup> h | π r l | πr (r + l) |
