## Venn Diagram

**BASICS OF VENN DIAGRAM -** In the chapter of the Venn diagram, we show the relationship between various objects, subjects, and numbers by the diagram. Generally, two types of questions are asked in this chapter.

## TYPE - 1

In this type of question some groups of objects are given and some diagrams are given. Through those diagrams one has to find that diagram which correctly classifies the group of objects in the given question. Let us understand these types of questions with the following example.

**Example- 1** Mango, potato, Book **Solution:-**

![](images/page_90_image_8.jpg)

Looking at the above group, it is clear that there is **no mutual relationship among the three.** Therefore, all three have been drawn differently.

**Example-2** India, Bihar, Patna **Solution:-**

![](images/page_90_image_11.jpg)

It is clear here that Patna is **part of** Bihar and Bihar is **part of** India. Hence the diagram is drawn as above.

**Example-3** Mango, Apple, Fruit **Solution:-**

![](images/page_90_image_14.jpg)

It is clear that Apple and Mango are **two different things,** but **both** are the **types of fruits,** so they are shown above.

**Example-4** Woman, Mother, Sister **Solution:-**

![](images/page_90_image_17.jpg)

It is clear that **some mothers** may be **sisters** and **some sisters** may also be **mothers**. Therefore the relation of mother and sister will be drawn from small circles and since **mother and sister are women**, the diagram will be shown from the above diagram.

**Example-5** Domestic animal, Dog, Cat **Solution:-**

![](images/page_90_image_21.jpg)

It is clear that **some dogs** and **some cats** are **pets,** but not **all pets** are **dogs and cats.** And dogs and cats are not related to each other, so the above question will be shown from this diagram.

**Example-6** Parents, Rich person, Farmers **Solution:-**

![](images/page_90_image_24.jpg)

It is clear that some parents can be rich and farmers too. Similarly, rich people can also be parents and farmers. Similarly, farmers can also be rich and parents. Hence: we will draw these three from this diagram.

**Example-7** Vegetable, Tomato, Orange **Solution:-**

![](images/page_90_image_27.jpg)

It is clear here that tomato is a type of vegetable. The relation of these two is shown by making two circles. But the orange class is completely different from both. Therefore it is drawn differently.

**Example-8** Snake, Reptiles, Poisonous **Solution:-**

![](images/page_90_image_30.jpg)

It is clear here that all snakes are reptiles. It is drawn by drawing two circles. But some snakes are also poisonous. Therefore, the person representing the poisons will be connected to these two circles. Hence the diagram will be drawn as above.

**Example-9** Woman, Mother, Baby girl **Solution:-**

![](images/page_90_image_33.jpg)

It is clear that all mothers are women. It is drawn by drawing two circles. Some women can also be children. But no child can be a mother.

**Example-10** Fathers , Uncles , Aunts **Solution:-**

![](images/page_90_image_36.jpg)

It is clear that some fathers can also be uncles and some uncles can also be fathers. But the class of aunt is completely different from these two. Hence: The diagram will be drawn as above.

In that figure, the letters or numbers are represented at different places which they represent to a particular class of letters or numbers.
