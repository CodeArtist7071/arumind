**Ex.(2).** The average of 9 numbers is 40. If the average of the first five numbers is 38 and the average of the last five numbers is 50, then what is the fifth number?

## Solution : -

The average of 9 numbers is 40. Total = 40 × 9 = 360. Average of the first five numbers is 38. Total = 38 × 5 = 190. Average of the last five numbers is 50. Total = 5 × 50 = 250. Fifth number = 190 + 250 - 360 = 440 - 360 = 80.
