## Decision Making

## What is Decision Making ?

It is a reasoning topic where candidates are tested on their ability to take logical, fair, and practical decisions in a given situation.A short case or scenario is presented (workplace, daily life, official setup), and you must choose the best possible decision from the options.It checks judgment, analytical ability, problem-solving, and fairness.

## 1. Nature of Questions

A situation/problem is described.

You are given 4 possible actions/ decisions.

You must identify which action is:

Most appropriate

Practical and fair

Consistent with rules/ethics

## 2. Key Features

Focus is on logical and balanced decision - making.Tests problem-solving ability under constraints.Involves ethical and unbiased choices.Often linked to workplace scenarios (HR, recruitment, official duties).

## 3. Common Types

**Eligibility-based decisions :-**

Like job/admission/loan criteria → check if candidate qualifies.

## Problem-solving decisions :-

Handling conflicts, disputes, or customer grievances.

**Ethical judgment :-** Choosing the morally correct and fair option.

Taking a decision that benefits the organization/team as a whole.

## 4. Approach to Solve

**Step 1 –** Read carefully: Understand the main issue/problem.

**Step 2 –** Identify the objective: What outcome is expected (fairness, rule-following, efficiency)?

**Step 3 –** Eliminate extremes: Avoid harsh, unfair, or impractical decisions.

**Step 4 –** Choose balanced action: The option that solves the problem logically and ethically.
