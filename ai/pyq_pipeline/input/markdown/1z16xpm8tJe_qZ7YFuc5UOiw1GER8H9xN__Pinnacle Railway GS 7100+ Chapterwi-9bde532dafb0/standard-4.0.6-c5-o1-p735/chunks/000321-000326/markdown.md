**Q.75.** Which of the following is an impact of river meandering in plains? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 13/08/2025 (Afternoon) (a) Formation of waterfalls (b) Formation of oxbow lakes (c) Formation of rift valleys (d) Formation of rapids

**Sol.75.(b) Formation of oxbow lakes.** In plains, rivers often meander due to gentle slopes and lateral erosion. Over time, the bends become exaggerated, and during floods, the river may cut through a narrow neck, creating a U-shaped oxbow lake. This process is typical in mature rivers flowing through alluvial plains, contributing to the dynamic landscape and soil fertility of floodplains.

**Q.76.** The concept of 'watershed' is primarily associated with: NTPC CBT - I (UG) 13/08/2025 (Afternoon) (a) Mountain formation (b) Climate zones

(c) River catchment area (d) Rainfall distribution

**Sol.76.(c) River catchment area.** A watershed is an area of land that channels all rainfall and snowmelt to a common outlet, such as a river, stream, lake, or ocean. It is also known as a drainage basin or catchment area. The boundaries of a watershed are defined by higher elevations, like hills or mountains, which direct the flow of water. Therefore, the concept of a watershed is fundamentally about the land area that collects and funnels water into a river system. States with renowned watershed management in India: Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Odisha, Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand.
