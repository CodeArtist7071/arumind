As a result, the cell begins to swell due to the intake of water. However, the plant cell does not burst because of the presence of a rigid cell wall that provides structural support. This swollen condition of the cell is called turgidity, which is important for maintaining the plant’s shape.

**Q.385.** Different types of permanent tissues arise as a result of RRB Tech. - (III) 30/12/2024 (Afternoon) (a) Elongation (b) Differentiation (c) Vegetative Propagation (d) Germination

**Sol.385.(b) Differentiation.** Permanent tissues are plant tissues that have lost the ability to divide and are specialized for specific functions. They are derived from meristematic tissues after differentiation. Permanent tissues are mainly classified into simple (parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma) and complex (xylem and phloem). These tissues help in support, storage, photosynthesis, and transport of water and nutrients. They form the major functional parts of a plant’s body.
