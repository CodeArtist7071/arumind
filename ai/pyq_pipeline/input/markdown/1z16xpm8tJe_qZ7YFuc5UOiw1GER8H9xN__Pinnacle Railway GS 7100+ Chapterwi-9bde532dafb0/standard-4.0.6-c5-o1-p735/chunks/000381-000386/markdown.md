## Volcano and Earthquake

**Q.665.** What evidence supports the layered structure of the Earth's interior? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 07/08/2025 (Afternoon) (a) Fossil records (b) Magnetic poles (c) Volcanic eruptions (d) Seismic wave behaviour

**Sol.665.(d) Seismic wave behaviour.**

Seismic waves from earthquakes reveal Earth’s structure. P-waves travel through solids, liquids, and gases but refract at layer boundaries, while S-waves travel only through solids—their absence in certain areas (S-wave shadow zone) shows a liquid outer core. These patterns confirm Earth’s layered structure: crust, mantle, outer core, and inner core.

**Q.666.** Volcanoes are often found in which type of plate boundary? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 08/08/2025 (Evening)

(a) Convergent and transform boundaries only

(b) Divergent boundaries only

(c) Convergent and divergent boundaries

(d) Transform boundaries only

**Sol.666.(c) convergent and divergent boundaries:** Convergent Boundaries (Subduction Zones): Oceanic plates subduct beneath continental or oceanic plates, melt, and form magma that rises to create volcanoes. Example: Pacific Ring of Fire - Andes Mountains, Japan, Philippines. Divergent Boundaries (Rift Zones): Plates move apart, magma fills the gap, forming new crust and volcanic activity. Example: Mid-Atlantic Ridge, East African Rift. Transform Boundaries: Mainly cause earthquakes, not volcanoes. Example: San Andreas Fault.

**Q.667.** What is the most common cause of tsunamis?

NTPC CBT - I (UG) 18/08/2025 (Afternoon)

(a) Volcanic eruptions only

(b) Excessive rainfall and flooding

(c) Undersea earthquakes

(d) Cyclones and high tides

**Sol.667.(c) Undersea earthquakes.** Tsunamis are large sea waves caused mainly by sudden underwater disturbances. The most common cause is undersea earthquakes, especially those along subduction zones where tectonic plates collide. Other causes include volcanic eruptions, underwater landslides, or asteroid impacts, but these are less frequent. When the sea floor suddenly moves vertically, it displaces huge volumes of water, generating waves that can travel across oceans at high speeds and cause devastating coastal damage.
