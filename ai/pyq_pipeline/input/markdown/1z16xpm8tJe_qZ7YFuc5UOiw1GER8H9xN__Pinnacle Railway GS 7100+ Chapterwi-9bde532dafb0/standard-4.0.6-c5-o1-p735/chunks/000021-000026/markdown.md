**Q.180.** How many major ragas are there in Carnatic music ? NTPC CBT - I (GL) 13/06/2025 (Evening) (a) 72 (b) 75 (c) 62 (d) 70

**Sol.180.(a) 72.** These are considered the "parent" ragas, or fundamental scales, from which all other ragas, known as Janya ragas, are derived. Each of the 72 Melakarta ragas contains all seven swaras (notes) in both its ascending (Arohana) and descending (Avarohana) scale. The 72 Melakarta ragas are organized into 12 groups called chakras, with each chakra containing six ragas. The first 36 Melakarta ragas use a natural fourth note (Sudha Madhyama), while the next 36 use an augmented fourth note (Prati Madhyama).
