**Q.605.** Which of the following was the final court of appeal for India under British rule? NTPC CBT- I (GL) 12/06/2025 (Afternoon) (a) Supreme Court at Calcutta (b) Federal Court (c) Privy Council in London (d) House of Commons

**Q.607.** The Mahalwari system was prevalent in certain regions of northern and central India. From whom was the land revenue collected under the Mahalwari system? NTPC CBT - I (GL) 13/06/2025 (Morning) (a) Village head (b) Individual peasants (c) Religious institutions (d) Landlords

**Sol.607.(a) Village head.** The Mahalwari system was introduced in 1822 by Holt Mackenzie and later modified by Lord William Bentinck. It was prevalent in Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and parts of Delhi. Under this system, land revenue was collected from the village (mahal) as a whole, with the village head (lambardar) responsible for payment to the government on behalf of all cultivators. Zamindari System (Permanent Settlement, 1793 Cornwallis): Revenue collected from zamindars (landlords), who acted as intermediaries between peasants and the British. Ryotwari System (introduced by Thomas Munro, 1820s): Revenue collected directly from individual peasants (ryots) without intermediaries.

**Q.611.** In 1820-21, which tribal group led a revolt against the authorities in the area now known as West Singhbhum district of Jharkhand, which was later suppressed by the British? NTPC CBT - I (GL) 14/06/2025 (Morning) (a) Munda tribes (b) Khasi tribes (c) Santhal tribes (d) Ho tribes

**Sol.611.(d) Ho tribes.** In 1820–21, the Ho tribes—an Adivasi (indigenous) community inhabiting the Singhbhum region (now part of West Singhbhum district in Jharkhand)—led a significant revolt against the expanding influence of colonial and feudal authorities. The uprising was primarily fueled by the British imposition of foreign administrative systems, heavy taxation, and the encroachment of their traditional lands and autonomy. Despite its suppression, the rebellion marked one of the earliest organized tribal resistances in colonial India, preceding other major uprisings like the Santhal Rebellion (1855) and the Munda Ulgulan (1899–1900).
