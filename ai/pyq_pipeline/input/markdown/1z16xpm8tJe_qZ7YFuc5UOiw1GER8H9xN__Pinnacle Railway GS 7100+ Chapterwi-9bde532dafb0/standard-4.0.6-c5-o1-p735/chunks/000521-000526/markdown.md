**Sol.835.(c) Sound waves in air.** Waves are classified as longitudinal or transverse based on the motion of particles relative to the direction of wave propagation. **Q.871.** Which of the following is a dimensionless quantity? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 13/08/2025 (Afternoon) (a) Velocity (b) Acceleration (c) Refractive index (d) Gravitational force

**Sol.871.(c) Refractive index.** A dimensionless quantity is a pure number without any physical units. For example, the refractive index is calculated as the ratio of the speed of light in vacuum to the speed of light in a medium. Since it is a ratio of two speeds, the units cancel out, making it dimensionless. In contrast, velocity (LT <sup>-1</sup> ), acceleration (LT <sup>-2</sup> ), and Gravitational force (MLT<sup>-2</sup> ) all have standard physical units.

**Q.872.** Which of the following physical quantities has zero dimension in mass and time? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 19/08/2025 (Morning) (a) Force (b) Work (c) Volume (d) Momentum

**Sol.872.(c) Volume.** Volume is a measure of space occupied by a body, expressed as length³ (L³). Its dimensional formula is [L³], meaning it involves only the length dimension and has zero dimensions in mass (M) and time (T). In contrast, Force [MLT⁻²], Work [ML²T⁻²], and Momentum [MLT⁻¹] involve mass and/or time, so they do not have zero dimensions in M and T. Volume is thus purely a geometrical quantity, independent of mass and temporal factors.
