NTPC CBT - I (GL) 16/06/2025 (Evening) (a) 2010 (b) 2000 (c) 2015 (d) 2004

**Sol.95.(d) 2004.** The Securities Transaction Tax (STT) was introduced in 2004 by Finance Minister P. Chidambaram to simplify taxation on securities, improve transparency, curb tax evasion, and prevent market manipulation.
