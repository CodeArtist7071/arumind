**Q.279.** The Indian textile industry was subjected to discriminatory trade practices. Which of the following policies harmed the Indian textile industry under British rule?

(a) Export subsidies

(b) Free trade within India

(c) Equal tariffs on imports and exports

(d) Imposition of heavy duties on Indian exports to Britain

**Sol.279.(d)** During British rule, India’s textile industry suffered due to discriminatory policies. British goods entered India at minimal or zero tariffs, flooding the market, while Indian textiles faced restrictions in Britain through high tariffs and bans, making them less competitive.
