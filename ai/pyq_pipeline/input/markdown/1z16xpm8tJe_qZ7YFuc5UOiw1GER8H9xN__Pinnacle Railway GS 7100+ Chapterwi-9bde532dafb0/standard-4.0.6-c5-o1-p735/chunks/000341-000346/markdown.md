**Sol.272.(b) Exfoliation** is a mechanical weathering process where outer layers of rock peel or flake off due to repeated temperature variations — typically heating during the day and cooling at night. **Q.294.** Littoral forests are most associated with which kind of ecosystem? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 22/08/2025 (Morning) (a) Wetland ecosystem (b) Tundra ecosystem (c) Grassland ecosystem (d) Desert ecosystem

**Sol.294.(a) Wetland ecosystem.** Littoral forests are coastal forests found along sandy shores, estuaries, and tidal regions, forming an important part of the wetland ecosystem. They act as buffers against coastal erosion, cyclones, and tsunamis and support rich biodiversity, including mangroves and salt-tolerant species. These forests thrive in areas with high moisture, tidal influence, and saline conditions. They are not associated with tundra, grassland, or desert ecosystems, which have entirely different climatic and soil conditions. Hence, littoral forests are characteristic of wetland ecosystems.
