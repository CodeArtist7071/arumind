**Q.285.** Which Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the impeachment procedure of the President? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 19/08/2025 (Evening) (a) Article 61 (b) Article 66 (c) Article 52 (d) Article 54

**Sol.285.(a) Article 61** : Procedure for impeachment of the President - The President can be impeached for constitutional violations. The procedure : A charge is initiated in either House with 14 days’ notice and signed by at least ¼ of members. The initiating House must pass the resolution by a 2/3 majority of total members. The other House investigates the charges; the President has the right to appear and be represented. If the investigating House sustains the charges by a 2/3 majority, the President is removed from office from the date of resolution.
