**Q.31.** If it is 12 noon at IST (82°30′E), what time is it approximately at 97°30'E longitude? NTPC CBT - I (GL) 18/06/2025 (Morning) (a) 1:00 PM (b) 1:20 PM (c) 11:00 AM (d) 12:30 PM

**Sol.31. (a) 1:00 PM.** Standard Meridian of India = 82°30′E (IST). Difference in longitude = 97°30′E – 82°30′E = 15° (eastward). For every 1° longitude → 4 minutes time difference. So, 15° × 4 minutes = 60 minutes (1 hour). Since longitude is east of IST, time will be ahead. Therefore, at 97°30′E, the time will be 1:00 PM when it is 12 noon at IST.
