**Q.384.** What is the direction of the Inter Tropical Convergence Zone in winter? RRC Group D 17/08/2022 (Evening) (a) Westward (b) Southward (c) Northward (d) Eastward

**Sol.384.(b) Southward.** The Inter Tropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ) follows the position of the sun and varies seasonally. In winter, due to the sun’s apparent movement toward the Tropic of Capricorn, the ITCZ moves **southward**, and so the reversal of wind direction takes place in the Indian Subcontinent. ITCZ is responsible for the wet and dry seasons in the tropics.
