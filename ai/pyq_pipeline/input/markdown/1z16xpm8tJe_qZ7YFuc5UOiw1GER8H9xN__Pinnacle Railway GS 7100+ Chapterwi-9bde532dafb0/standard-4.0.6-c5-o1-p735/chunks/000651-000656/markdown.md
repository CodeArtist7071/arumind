## Reproduction in Plant

**Q.237.** Which of the following organs represents the female reproductive part of a flower in angiosperms ? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 08/08/2025 (Evening) (a) Petal (b) Androecium (c) Gynoecium (d) Stamen

**Sol.237.(c) Gynoecium.** In angiosperms (flowering plants), the female reproductive part of a flower is called the gynoecium. The gynoecium consists of one or more carpels, each having: Stigma Receives pollen. Style - Connects stigma to ovary. Ovary - Contains ovules (future seeds). Together, these structures are responsible for fertilization and seed development. Petal - Brightly colored part of a flower that attracts pollinators. Androecium - Represents the male reproductive part of a flower. Stamen - Individual unit of the androecium (male reproductive organ).

**Q.238.** Which of the following parts of the plant is primarily responsible for sexual reproduction in angiosperms ? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 12/08/2025 (Evening)

(a) Stem (b) Leaf (c) Flower (d) Root

**Sol.238.(c) Flower.** In angiosperms, sexual reproduction occurs through flowers, which are the reproductive organs of the plant. Flowers contain the male reproductive structure (stamens) that produce pollen grains and the female reproductive structure (carpels/pistils) that contain ovules. Pollination transfers pollen to the stigma, leading to fertilization inside the ovary where seeds form. The other plant parts — stem, leaf, and root — primarily perform functions like support, photosynthesis, and absorption, but they do not play a direct role in sexual reproduction.

**Q.239.** In angiosperms, which of the following organs represents the male reproductive part of a flower? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 22/08/2025 (Evening) (a) Gynoecium (b) Petals (c) Androecium (d) Sepals

**Sol.239.(c) Androecium.** In angiosperms (flowering plants), the androecium is the male reproductive part of the flower. It consists of stamens, each having a filament and an anther, where pollen grains (male gametes) are produced. The gynoecium is the female reproductive part, comprising carpels or pistils. Petals are colorful structures that attract pollinators, and sepals protect the flower bud.

**Q.240.** Which of the following characteristics corresponding to plant functions is true for angiosperms ? RPF Constable 17/03/2025 (Morning) (a) They reproduce using spores. (b) They predominantly rely on wind for pollination. (c) They lack vascular tissue for nutrient transport. (d) They produce seeds enclosed within fruits.

**Sol.240.(d) They produce seeds enclosed within fruits.** Angiosperms, or flowering plants, are unique for producing flowers that house reproductive organs. After fertilization, the ovary forms a fruit to protect and spread seeds. They are mainly classified as monocots (one cotyledon) and dicots (two cotyledons). Further grouping is based on growth habit (herbaceous or woody) and life cycle (annual, biennial, or perennial).

**Q.241.** Which reproductive part(s) is/are essential for fertilisation ? RRB Tech. - (III) 20/12/2024 (Afternoon) (a) Sepal (b) Thalamus (c) Petals (d) Pollen grain and ovule

**Sol.241.(d) Pollen grain and ovule.** Fertilization in plants occurs when the female gamete (ovum or egg) fuses with the male gamete (sperm). Both the ovum and sperm are haploid, containing one set of chromosomes, and their fusion forms a diploid zygote with two sets of chromosomes, which later develops into the mature plant (sporophyte).
