**Sol.425.(d) Economic exploitation of India.** The main motive of British colonial rule in India was the economic exploitation of the country to serve British interests. Policies such as high land revenue (Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari, Mahalwari), deindustrialisation of Indian handicrafts, and control of trade were designed to extract wealth and raw materials for Britain. Social reforms or industrialisation were secondary and often incidental. The welfare of Indian farmers and social development were largely neglected.

Therefore, the primary goal of colonial rule was to maximize revenue and economic benefits for Britain.

(a) strengthen colonial rule

(b) support women empowerment

(c) expand Indian trade

(d) reform Indian society

**Sol.431.(a) Strengthen colonial rule.** The British introduced modern education in India primarily to strengthen colonial control. Their aim was to create a class of educated Indians who would serve as loyal clerks, administrators, and intermediaries between rulers and the masses.
