**Q.2.** Which Indian athlete won gold in the women's high jump at the 2025 Asian Athletics Championships ? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 08/08/2025 (Morning) (a) Sahana Kumari (b) Nandini Agasara (c) Pooja Singh (d) Swapna Barman

**Sol.2.(c) Pooja Singh.** Pooja is only the second Indian woman ever to win gold in the women’s high jump at the Asian Athletics Championships. The previous was Bobby Aloysius, who won in 2000. By clearing 1.89m, she improved over her previous U20 record (1.85m) and moved very close to the senior record of 1.92m.

**Q.3.** In 2025, which city in France hosted the French Open tennis tournament ? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 08/08/2025 (Evening) (a) Lyon (b) Marseille (c) Paris (d) Nice

**Sol.3.(c)** The French Open, also called Roland Garros, is held annually in Paris at the Stade Roland Garros. It is one of the four Grand Slam tennis tournaments, the only one played on clay courts. Roland Garros Stadium has been the host since 1928. The tournament attracts top-ranked players worldwide and is a significant event on the international tennis calendar.

**Q.5.** Which country served as the host for the 2025 Asian Winter Games? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 11/08/2025 (Afternoon) (a) Kazakhstan (b) China (c) Japan (d) South Korea

**Sol.5.(b) China.** The 2025 Asian Winter Games were held in Harbin, China, from February 7 to 14, marking the second time the city hosted the event. **Q.6.** Puja Tomar, the first Indian woman to achieve a victory in a UFC (Ultimate Fighting Championship) fight in 2024, participates in which sport? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 11/08/2025 (Afternoon) (a) Wrestling (b) Mixed Martial Arts (c) Boxing (d) Judo

**Sol.6.(b) Mixed Martial Arts.** In 2024, Puja Tomar created history by becoming the first Indian woman to win a fight in the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC), the world’s leading Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) competition. She defeated Brazil’s Rayanne dos Santos in her debut match, marking a significant milestone for Indian combat sports. MMA is a full-contact sport that integrates techniques from boxing, wrestling, judo, karate, and jiu-jitsu.

**Sol.7.(d) Gajsimha.** Bihar hosted the 7ᵗʰ Khelo India Youth Games (KIYG) 2025 from 4 to 15 May across Patna, Gaya, Rajgir, Begusarai, and Bhagalpur. The mascot, “Gajsimha,” inspired by the Pala dynasty heritage of Nalanda and Bodhgaya, represents the strength of an elephant and the courage of a lion. **Q.8.** What was the name of stadium where India beat Bangladesh in South Asian Football Federation (SAFF) Under-19 Championship 2025? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 12/08/2025 (Afternoon)

(a) JRD Tata Sports Complex, Jamshedpur

(b) Yupia Outdoor Stadium, Arunachal Pradesh

(c) Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium, Delhi

(d) Salt Lake Stadium, Kolkata

**Sol.8.(b) Yupia Outdoor Stadium, Arunachal Pradesh.** The 2025 SAFF U-19 Championship final, where India defeated Bangladesh 4–3 in a penalty shootout after a 1–1 draw in regular time, was held at the Golden Jubilee Stadium in Yupia, Arunachal Pradesh. This venue marked a significant milestone as it hosted the first international football tournament of this scale in the region. The stadium's capacity is 15,000, and it is operated by the Arunachal Pradesh Football Association.

**Q.9.** Which country won both the Men's and Women's titles at the Kabaddi World Cup 2025, held at Wolverhampton in England? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 12/08/2025 (Afternoon) (a) Iran (b) Pakistan (c) Bangladesh (d) India

**Sol.9.(d) India** made history by winning both the Men's and Women's titles at the Kabaddi World Cup 2025, held in Wolverhampton, England. The Men's team narrowly defeated England 44–41, while the Women's team secured a commanding 57–34 victory over the hosts. This tournament marked the first time the Kabaddi World Cup was held outside Asia, with matches hosted across Birmingham, Coventry, Walsall, and Wolverhampton.
