**Q.300.** Which of the following architectures has been built in the 'Charbagh' style? NTPC CBT - I (GL) 10/06/2025 (Afternoon) (a) Qutub Minar (b) Taj Mahal (c) Gumbaj of Bijapur (d) Quwwat-ul-Islam mosque

**Sol.300.(b) Taj Mahal.** The Charbagh is a Persian-style garden layout that divides the garden into four parts using walkways or flowing water, symbolizing the four gardens of Paradise mentioned in the Quran. This style was adopted and perfected in Mughal architecture, with the Taj Mahal being the most iconic example. The entire complex of the Taj Mahal is designed around a symmetrical Charbagh garden, enhancing its aesthetic appeal and spiritual symbolism. This layout reflects the Mughal emphasis on harmony, symmetry, and beauty, which are key characteristics of their architectural masterpieces.
