**Q.106.** The Mauryan period saw the development of which script for inscriptions ? NTPC CBT - I (GL) 21/06/2025 (Morning) (a) Brahmi (b) Grantha (c) Kharosthi (d) Pali

**Sol.106.(a) Brahmi**. The Mauryan period, particularly during the reign of Emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE, saw the widespread use of the Brahmi script for inscriptions. Ashoka’s edicts, which were inscribed on pillars, rocks, and caves across the Indian subcontinent, were primarily written in Brahmi in the eastern and southern parts of his empire. While Kharosthi was also used during this time, it was limited to the northwestern regions. Grantha developed much later in South India, and Pali is a language, not a script.
