**Sol.374.(d) Governor of the State.** The Chief Minister (CM) is the real executive head of a state government in India and is primarily responsible to the Governor. Under Article 164 of the Indian Constitution, the Governor appoints the CM, and the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the state legislative assembly.
