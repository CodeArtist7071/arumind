**Q.19.** When you look at the front side of a tablespoon, an inverted image of yours is formed. The front side of table spoon works as a: RRB Tech. - (III) 24/12/2024 (Morning) (a) plane mirror (b) concave lens (c) convex mirror (d) concave mirror

**Sol.19.(d) concave mirror.** A concave mirror forms an inverted image when the object is placed beyond the focal point. When you look at the front side of the tablespoon from a distance, your image appears inverted and real due to the converging nature of concave mirrors. Hence, the front side of the spoon acts as a concave mirror.
