**Q.517.** Which reformer gave the slogan 'One God, One Caste'? NTPC CBT- I (GL) 12/06/2025 (Afternoon) (a) Jyotiba Phule (b) Keshub Chandra Sen (c) Narayana Guru (d) Dayanand Saraswati

**Sol.517.(c) Narayana Guru.** Narayana Guru, IPA, was a philosopher, spiritual leader, and social reformer in India. He led a reform movement against injustice in the caste-ridden society of Kerala in order to promote spiritual enlightenment and social equality. He was born on 20 August 1856, Chempazhanthy in Thiruvananthapuram, and died on 20 September 1928, in Sivagiri, Kerala. **Q.518.** The Paramhansa Sabha was founded in: NTPC CBT - I (GL) 14/06/2025 (Morning) (a) 1865 (b) 1849 (c) 1857 (d) 1875

**Sol.518.(b) 1849.** The Paramhansa Sabha—also known as the Paramahansa Mandali—was founded in 1849 in Bombay (present-day Mumbai) as a covert socio‑religious reform society. Led by reformers such as Dadoba Pandurang and Durgaram Mehtaji, the group sought to challenge entrenched caste norms, promote monotheism, advocate for widow remarriage, and support women’s education. They even practiced communal meals prepared by lower-caste cooks to symbolize equality — an act that was considered radical at the time.

**Q.520.** ‘Tuhfat-ul-Muwahhidin’ was written by: NTPC CBT - I (GL) 16/06/2025 (Evening) (a) Akshay Kumar Dutt (b) Keshub Chandra Sen (c) Rammohan Roy (d) Sayyid Ahmed Khan

**Sol.520.(c) Rammohan Roy.** The work ‘Tuhfat-ul-Muwahhidin’, which translates to ‘A Gift to Monotheists’, was written by Raja Rammohan Roy in 1804. This treatise reflects his deep commitment to monotheism and his opposition to religious orthodoxy and superstition. Written in Persian with an Arabic preface, the book criticizes idol worship and advocates for the worship of a single, formless God. It is one of his earliest known writings and lays the foundation for his later reformist activities, including the Brahmo Samaj movement, which aimed at religious and social reform in India.

**Q.521.** Who among the following was a radical rationalist and iconoclast in Maharashtra? NTPC CBT - I (GL) 17/06/2025 (Evening) (a) Vishnushastri Chiplunkar (b) Gopal Ganesh Agarkar (c) Bhaskar Pandurang (d) Ganesh Vasudev Joshi

**Sol.521.(b) Gopal Ganesh Agarkar** was a prominent social reformer, thinker, and educationist from Maharashtra in the 19th century. He was known for his radical rationalist views and strong opposition to blind faith, superstition, and traditional social customs. As an iconoclast, he challenged established norms and advocated for social reforms based on reason, equality, and individual liberty. He was a close associate of Bal Gangadhar Tilak initially, but their differing views on the priority of political vs. social reforms led to their eventual divergence. Agarkar emphasized the importance of social reforms, education, and rational thinking for the upliftment of society.
