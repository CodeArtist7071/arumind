**Q.332.** Which of the following Acts provides legal status to Lok Adalats in India? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 14/08/2025 (Evening) (a) Indian Penal Code, 1860 (b) Civil Procedure Code, 1908 (c) Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 (d) Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987

**Sol.332.(d) The Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987**. Lok Adalats are alternative dispute resolution forums that help resolve cases quickly, affordably, and amicably without going through lengthy court procedures. Under this Act, the decisions (awards) of Lok Adalats are deemed to be final and binding, with the same status as a civil court decree. The Act ensures that even marginalized sections of society have access to justice, in line with the constitutional mandate of providing free legal aid and promoting justice on the basis of equal opportunity.
