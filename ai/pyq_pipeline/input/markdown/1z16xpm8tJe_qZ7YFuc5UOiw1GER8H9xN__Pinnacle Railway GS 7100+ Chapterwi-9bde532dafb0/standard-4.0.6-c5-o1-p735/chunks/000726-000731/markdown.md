This forms sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄) and nitric acid (HNO₃), which fall as acid rain, leading to soil degradation, water pollution, and damage to buildings and crops. Controlling SO₂ and NOₓ emissions is crucial to reducing acid rain and its environmental impact.
