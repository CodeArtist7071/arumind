**Q.43.** Which of the four Vedas is traditionally paired with the Upaveda known as Gandharvaveda? NTPC CBT - I (UG) 03/09/2025 (Evening) (a) Yajurveda (b) Atharvaveda (c) Samaveda (d) Rigveda

**Sol.43.(c) Samaveda.** The Gandharvaveda is an Upaveda (a secondary Veda) associated with the Samaveda. The term "Gandharva" refers to music and the arts, making this pairing logical, as the Samaveda is known for its melodic and musical aspects. The Yajurveda is associated with the Upaveda Dhanurveda, which deals with archery and warfare. The Atharvaveda is linked with various Upavedas, including Arthashastra, which focuses on economics, statecraft, and governance. The Rigveda, the oldest Veda, primarily contains hymns and praises of deities.

**Q.46.** Which of the following is NOT one of the primary tones used in Vedic chanting ? NTPC CBT - I (GL) 12/06/2025 (Morning) (a) Anudaatta (b) Svarita (c) Mandrata (d) Udatta

**Q.47.** Which traditional method of Vedic chanting is regarded as the most complex and extensive among all the recitation styles? NTPC CBT - I (GL) 24/06/2025 (Evening) (a) Ghanapatha (b) Shikhapatha (c) Dandapatha (d) Jatapatha

**Sol.47. (a) Ghanapatha.** Among the various Vedic recitation styles (pathas), Ghanapatha is considered the most complex and extensive. In this method, words of the Vedic hymns are chanted in a specific forward and backward sequence, creating a rhythmic and elaborate pattern. This style ensures both the preservation of phonetics and the accuracy of Vedic texts, making it one of the highest forms of oral tradition in India. Shikhapatha - Simplified linear recitation. Dandapatha - Words recited in pairs, less complex. Jatapatha Medium-level complexity; words recited forward and backward, but not as elaborate as Ghanapatha.
