## Data Sufficiency

## Concept :- What is data sufficiency?

In questions based on Data Sufficiency, candidates are given a piece of information followed by a few conditions and then a few options where they have to answer which of the given conditions shall be necessary to answer the question.

Understanding the answer option.

In Data sufficiency problems, a question consists of two statements labeled I and II, in which a certain information is given. You have to decide whether the information given in the statements is sufficient to answer the question or not. Using the information given in the statements you will have to state that.

(a) Both statements 1 and 2 are

suffi cient

(b) Statement 2 alone is sufficient while 1 alone is insufficient

(c) Statement 1 alone is sufficient while statement 2 alone is insufficient

(d) Either statement 1 or 2 is sufficient

(e) Neither statement 1 nor 2 is sufficient to answer the question.

## Tricks to solve data sufficiency:-

**Step 1 : -** Examine the question, what is asked? Do we have to find a value or do we have to check the relationship? Do not look for the final answer (if not asked). You mostly have to only answer which conditions will satisfy your requirements to meet the answer to the question

**Step 2 : -** Consider each statement individually. First look at statement A. Does it provide, all by itself enough information to answer the question? If so, then check statement B if it is also providing enough information then answer will be either statement 1 or 2 is sufficient. If not then our answer will be statement 1 alone is sufficient while statement 2 alone is insufficient. If statement A is not providing enough information but statement B is providing enough information then our answer will be statement 2 alone is sufficient while 1 alone is insufficient .

If neither of the statements by itself is sufficient to answer the question, consider whether you can answer the question by combining the information given in both statements. Then our answer will be Both statements 1 and 2 are sufficient.

**Direction: [ Q .1. - Q .67. ]** - A question is given, followed by some statements .

Identify which of the statement(s) is/are sufficient to answer the question.

(I) Exactly three people sit between B and C.

(II) A sits immediately to the left of B.

RRB J.E. 18/12/2024 (Evening)

(a) Both Statements I and II put together are not sufficient to answer the question.

(b) Data in Statement II alone is sufficient to answer the question, while data in Statement I is not.

(c) Data in Statement I alone is sufficient to answer the question, while data in Statement II is not.

(d) Both Statements I and II put together (and not independently) are sufficient to answer the question.
