## Completion of Figures

**Introduction of completion of figure -**

In this type of problem, a figure or a matrix, containing a set of figures following particular sequences or patterns is given, in which a part, generally a quarter, is left blank. This problem figure is followed by four alternative figures. The candidate is required to select the one which best fits into the blank space of the problem figure so as to complete the original pattern.

## Mirror / Water Image

## BASICS OF MIRROR IMAGE-

The image that is formed in the mirror of an object is called the mirror image of the object. In this, the image of the object which is near the mirror is formed first in the mirror and the image which is away from the mirror is formed later in the mirror.

## BASICS OF WATER IMAGE-

A water image is the reflection of an object in water, and it is the inverted image of an object turned over on its side. Using a horizontal mirror at the bottom of a figure's water image creates a mirror image of that figure.
