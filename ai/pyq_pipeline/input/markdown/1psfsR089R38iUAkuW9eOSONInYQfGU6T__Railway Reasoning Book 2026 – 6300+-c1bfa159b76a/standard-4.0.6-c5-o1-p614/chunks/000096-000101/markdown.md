## Cube and Dice

**BASICS OF DICE** - Dice is a three-dimensional shape with a surface whose length, width and height are equal. Its 3 surfaces are always visible and three are not visible. Numbers or dots or different shapes are represented on its surfaces from 1 to 6.

**Classification of dice :-**

![](images/page_100_image_6.jpg)

There are two types of dice. **(i)** Base Dice **(ii)** Open Dice Base Dice further classified into two type -

## Type - 1 → Base Dice :-

**(i) Standard Dice**- A dice whose sum of digits on opposite surfaces is always 7 and the sum of digits on any two adjacent surfaces is not 7.

![](images/page_100_image_10.jpg)

Here, 1 + 2 ≠ 7 1 + 4 ≠ 7

**(ii) General Dice**- A dice in which the sum of the numbers on any two adjacent surfaces is 7, it is a common dice.

![](images/page_100_image_13.jpg)

Here,

$$
\left[ 2 + 5 = 7 \right],
$$

This would be a common dice as it has 2 on the adjacent surface of 5 and the adjacent can not be opposite. Therefore, it is a common dice and may contain digits marked on opposite surfaces of the digit. like -

On the opposite surface of 2 → may be 1/3/6. On the opposite surface of 5 → may be 1/3/6 On the opposite surface of 4 → may be 1/3/6

Rules for solving dice related questions. 1. Rules of standardisation. 2. Rule of adjacent digit/figure/symbol. 3. Rule of common digit, figure, symbol.

4. Rule of square circular motion.

## RULE OF STANDARDISATION -

According to the law of standardisation, if a dice whose sum of two adjacent digits, present in any shape is not 7, then the sum of the digits on two opposite surfaces of such a pass will always be 7.

![](images/page_100_image_22.jpg)

Here we see that the sum of any two adjacent digits of a given dice is not 7, thus this dice is the standard dice and the sum of digits of opposite surfaces is always 7.

If a digit is inscribed on a surface of a standard dice, it will have the following digit on its opposite surface.

On the opposite surface of $1 = 6(1 + 6 = 7)$ On the opposite surface of 2 = 5(2 + 5= 7) On the opposite surface of 3 = 4 (3 + 4= 7) On the opposite surface of 4 = 3 (3 + 4= 7) On the opposite surface of $5 = 2(5 + 2 = 7)$ On the opposite surface of $6 = 1 ( 1 + 6 = 7 )$
