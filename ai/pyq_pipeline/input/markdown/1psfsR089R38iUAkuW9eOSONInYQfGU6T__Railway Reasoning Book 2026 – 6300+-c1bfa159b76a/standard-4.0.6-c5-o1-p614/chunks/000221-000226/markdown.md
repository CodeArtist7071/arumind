## Clock

**Concept : -** A clock is a complete circle having 360 degrees. It is divided into 12 equal parts i.e. each part is 360/12 = 30°. As the minute hand takes a complete round in one hour, it covers 360° in 60 minutes. In 1 minute it covers 360/60 = 6°/ minute. Similarly, an hour hand covers 1 hour with a speed of 0.5 °/ minute.

1. Time is given → Angle is asked 2. Angle is given → Time is asked 3. Image based question 4. Error in the clock

⇒ There are two types of hand in the clock, one is the hour hand and other is the minute hand. ⇒ The relative speed of minute hand w.r.t hour hand = 5.5°

**Type 1.** Time is given → Angle is asked The formula of this type of question is $30H-\frac{11M}{2}= A n g l e$

**Ex.** What will be the angle made by the hands of the clock at 7: 20 ? Ans - Put ‘7’ at the place of H and ‘20’ at the place of M. 30 $\times 7 - \frac{11 \times 20}{2} = 100^{\circ}$

**Type 2.** Angle is given → Time is asked How many times needles will meet each other at 0° , 90° , 180°

| Degree | In 1 hour | 12 hour | 24 hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0° | 1 time | 11 times | 22 times |
| 90° | 2 times | 22 times | 44 times |
| 180° | 1 time | 11 times | 22 times |

**Formula for this type of question is :-** $\frac{2}{11} \left[ \left| \mathbf{H} \times 30 + \mathbf{A} \right| \right]$

Here T = Time at which angle is made H = Hour hand A = Angle

**Ex.** At what time between 4 and 5 o’clock, the hands make an angle of 10 degrees? Ans. Putting the formula =

$$
\mathsf {T} = \frac {2}{1 1} [ \mathsf {H} \times 3 0 + / - \mathsf {A} ]
$$

**Note :-** When the hour hand is in the first half we take ‘+’ sign and when the hour hand is in the second half we take ‘-’ sign.

$$
\mathrm{T} = \frac {2}{1 1} [ 4 \times 3 0 + 1 0 ] \Rightarrow \mathrm{T} = \frac {2}{1 1} [ 1 3 0 ]
$$

$$
\left| T = \frac {2 6 0}{1 1} \Rightarrow T = 2 3 \frac {7}{1 1} \right|
$$

**Type 3.** Image based question In this type of question an image of a clock is given with a certain time and we have to answer what will be its mirror or water image.

**Ex.** A clock, when seen from the mirror, shows time 8:20. What is the real time in the clock? Ans. Formula :- For mirror image ⇒ (11− hours) : (60 −minutes) $\Rightarrow ( 1 1 - 8 ) : ( 6 0 - 2 0 ) \Rightarrow 3 : 4 0$

**Type 4.** Error based question Ex. A clock becomes 12s fast in every 3h. If it is made correct at 3 O’ clock in the afternoon of sunday, then what time will it show at 10 O’ clock Tuesday morning? **Ans.** Total time from 3 O’ clock sunday afternoon to 10 O’ clock tuesday morning = 43h Total increased time $\frac { 1 2 } { 3 } \times 4 3$ = 172s = 2min 52s
