## Alphanumeric Series

**What is an alphanumeric series?** Alphanumeric series questions are a combination of numbers, alphabets and symbol-based series which candidates need to answer the questions based on the series.

## Types of series:-

**1. Alphanumeric symbol series :-** This series is a combination of symbol, alphabet and number. **Ex -** (Left) S 7 & D M # Z 6% 3 T 2 \* H G = C ? 4 Y B (Right)

**2.Alphanumeric series:** This series is a combination of alphabet and numbers. **Ex** - (Left) G 7 A D 6 1 R 5 S 7 T U 2 3 A Y (Right)

**3. Alphabetic Series:** This type of series consists of only the alphabet . **Ex-**(Left) CAT OWN EYE AND SAD (Right)

**Ex. -** (Left) 534 945 317 354 781 (Right) Questions based on this type can be asked in the form of rearrangement of numbers or digits in ascending, descending or any other order.

## Some Example to understand :-

**Ex.1.** (Left) A 9 % B @ 6 & L # K 9 @ 7 # 6 K M \* 7 & B (Right) How many such letters are there that are immediately preceded by a symbol and also immediately followed by a number? (a) 4 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 5

**Sol .** Here, we have to find the sequence as “symbol, then letter, and then number”. In this type of question always start with which comes first in the sequence. In this case the symbol is first in the sequence, so we will start with the symbol. Therefore, only # K 9 satisfies.

**Ex.2.**(Left) A 9 % B @ 6 & L # K 9 @ 7 # 6 K M \* 7 & B (Right) Which of the following is the seventh to the right of the twentieth from the right in the above arrangement?

**Concept**-If the question says two things from the same side (both from right or both from left), then subtract the place values. Example: 20 − 7 = 13th from right. If the question says one from left and one from right, then add the place values. Example: 20 + 7.

**Ex.3.** If we remove all the vowels in the series then which of the following element will be 7th from right end (Left) E # R & H & @ J & M N # B & V && D # P @ W % & Q & A % & # E & (Right)

**Concept-** Some students make a mistake that they write a series after dropping the vowels and then count the elements but you don’t have to follow this approach. Just imagine that vowels are not present in the series and then count the elements
