## Profit and Loss

**1.** Profit incurs when SP (selling price) is more than CP (cost price).

**2.** Profit = SP – CP

**3.** Loss incurs when CP is more than SP i.e. Loss = CP – SP 4. % $\mathrm{Profit}=\frac{\mathrm{Profit}}{\mathrm{CP}} \times 100$ 5. % $\frac{loss}{CP} \times 100$

**6.** Profit or Loss is always calculated on cost price.

$$
SP = CP \times \frac{100 \pm profit / loss \%}{100}
$$

$$
CP = SP \times \frac{100}{100 \pm profit / loss \%}
$$

Note: In case of profit you must add and in case of loss subtract in above two formulas.
