## Solutions :-

**Sol.1.(b) (2)** - were ready for the sudden fire drill 'Were' should be replaced with 'was'. When 'neither... nor connects two subjects, the verb agrees with the subject closest to it. In this case, 'the teacher' is singular, so the verb should be singular ('was'). Hence, 'was ready for the sudden fire drill' is the most appropriate answer.

**Sol.2.(b) (2)** - remains a contentious subject ‘Remains’ must be replaced with ‘remain’. The subject of the sentence is ‘The profound implications’ (plural). The phrase ‘a field still largely inscrutable to the uninitiated’ provides additional information about ‘quantum theories’ but does not change the number of the main subject. Therefore, a plural verb is required. Hence, ‘remain a contentious subject’ is the most appropriate answer.
