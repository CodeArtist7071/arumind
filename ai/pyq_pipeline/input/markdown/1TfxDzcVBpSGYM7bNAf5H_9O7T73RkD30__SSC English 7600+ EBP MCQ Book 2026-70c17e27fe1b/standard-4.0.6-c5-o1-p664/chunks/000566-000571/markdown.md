**Sol.101.(c) Revive -** to bring back to life or consciousness; to make active or strong again.

**Wilt -** to become weak or limp (often used for plants when they lose water); to lose strength or energy.

**Cruel -** causing pain or suffering on purpose; unkind and harsh.

**Merciless -** having no pity; showing no mercy or compassion.

**Strict -** demanding that rules are followed exactly; firm and not flexible.
