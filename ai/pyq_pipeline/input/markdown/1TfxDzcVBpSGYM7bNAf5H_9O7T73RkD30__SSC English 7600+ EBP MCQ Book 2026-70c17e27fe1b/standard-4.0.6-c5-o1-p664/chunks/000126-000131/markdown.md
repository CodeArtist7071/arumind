## Sol.690.(b) Because he has been in custody since last July

“Since” is used to denote a point of time and for depicting an activity that is happening from a long time like since time immemorial/since ancient times. “For” is used for a fixed period of time(countable) like 2 years/3 months/4 days/8 hours and for an activity that happened in a recent time. Hence, ‘Because he has been in custody since last July’ is the most appropriate answer.
