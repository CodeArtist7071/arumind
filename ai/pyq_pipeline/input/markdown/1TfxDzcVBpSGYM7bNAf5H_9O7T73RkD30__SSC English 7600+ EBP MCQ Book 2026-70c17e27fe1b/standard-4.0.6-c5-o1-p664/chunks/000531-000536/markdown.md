**Sol.403.(c) Widespread**- existing or occurring over a large area or affecting many people or things. **Pervasive**- spreading widely throughout an area or a group of people. **Equatorial**- of, at or near the equator.

**Retrospective**- looking back on or considering past events, actions, or experiences. **Longitudinal**- relating to the length or direction from one end to the other.

**Sol.404.(b) Unavoidable**- something that cannot be prevented. **Inevitable**- something that is bound to occur or cannot be avoided. **Impassionate**- lacking passion or strong emotion. **Amiable**- friendly and pleasant in manner. **Measurable**- capable of being measured or quantified.

**Sol.405.(c) Ancient**- extremely old. **Antique**- an object or item that is valuable because of its old age. **Gothic**- relating to the architectural and artistic style of the Middle Ages. **New**- recently created or made. **Modern**- pertaining to the present time or recent times.

**Sol.406.(d) Loyal**- showing firm and constant support to a person, group, or cause. **Faithful**- reliable and consistent in one's loyalty or commitment. **Cheerful**- happy and positive. **Unfaithful**- not loyal or reliable. **Daring**- willing to take risks or face challenges.

**Sol.407.(a)** Yearning- keenly desiring.

**Sol.408.(b) Furious**- extremely angry or filled with rage.

**Fierce**- intensely strong or aggressive. **Strange**- unusual or unfamiliar.

**Lame**- unable to walk properly due to an injury or disability. **Lavish**- very generous or liberal in giving or spending.

**Sol.409.(d)** Trousseau - the clothes, linen, and other belongings collected by a bride for her marriage.

**Sol.410.(d) Excruciating**- extremely painful or agonizing. **Harrowing**- causing deep emotional pain. **Exterminate**- to completely destroy or eliminate something.

**Awesome**- extremely impressive or remarkable.

**Sad**- feeling or expressing unhappiness or sorrow.

**Sol.411.(c) Remembrance**- the act of recalling or thinking about something from the past. **Reminiscence**- the process of remembering past events or experiences. **Harsh**- severe or rough in manner or

appearance. **Oblivion**- the state of being forgotten or completely unaware. **Benevolence**- the quality of being kind, charitable, and generous.

**Sol.412.(d) Reinstall**- to install something again, often referring to software or equipment. **Rehabilitate**- to restore to good health, condition, or normal life. **Absolve**- to free or pardon from guilt, blame, or responsibility. **Develop**- to create or improve something over time. **Decline**- to move in a downward direction or experience a reduction. **Sol.413.(c)** Prowess exceptional mastery.

**Sol.414.(b)** Familiar- known.

**Sol.415.(c) Empirical**- based on observation, experience, or evidence rather than theory or pure logic. **Factual**- based on facts. **Evidence**- information or proof that supports or confirms a belief, theory, or claim. **Hypothesis**- an idea which is suggested as a possible explanation for a particular situation or condition. **Rigorous**- requiring great attention to detail and precision.

**Malicious**- intending to do harm. **Diabolical**- extremely bad or shocking. **Sophisticated**- having or showing much worldly knowledge or cultural experience. **Sycophant**- a person who flatters and admires important people in order to gain favor or advantage. **Celestial**- of or relating to heaven or the sky.

**Sol.417.(b) Considerable**- notably large in size, amount, or extent. **Major**- large or important. **Creviced**- having many narrow openings or cracks. **Diligent**- showing hard work and attention to detail. **Shallow**- of little depth and not deep.

**Sol.418.(a) Vagabond**- a person who has no home and wanders from place to place. **Vagrancy**- the state of living as a vagrant or homeless. **Vagueness**- the state of being unclear or inexact. **Fragrance**- a pleasant smell. **Exploration**- the activity of searching and finding out about something.
