## Sol.39.(b) so that he might pass

‘So that’ is used to indicate the purpose or intention behind an action. ‘Might’ is used to indicate a possibility in the past. As per the given sentence, it was the intention of the student to pass the difficult examination. Hence, ‘so that he might’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.40.(b) for five years

‘For’ is used to indicate a fixed period of time (countable), such as two years, three months, four days, or eight hours. It shows how long an action has been continuing. On the other hand, ‘since’ is used for a specific point in time like since 2020, since Monday, or since morning. In the given sentence, “five years” refers to a fixed duration, so “for” is the correct choice. Hence, “for five years” is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.41.(c) debated

The verb 'debate' is transitive and usually does not require a preposition when followed directly by its object. Using 'about', 'on', 'over', or 'regarding' after 'debate' is grammatically incorrect. Hence, ‘debated the new policy’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.42.(b) for two hours

‘For’ is used for a fixed duration. ‘Since’ is used to denote a point of time in the past. Here, ‘two hours’ is a fixed duration. Hence, ‘for two hours’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.43.(a) superior to

Adjectives like 'superior', 'inferior', 'junior', 'senior', 'prior', and 'preferable' are followed by the preposition 'to', when making comparisons. Hence, ‘superior to’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.44.(b) reverted

The word “reverted” itself means to go back to a previous state or habit, so using “back” is redundant (unnecessary repetition). Hence, “reverted” is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.45.(d) were presenting their findings at the conference

The verb ‘went out’ indicates the action was happening in the past, not the present. Here, the power went out which interrupted the presentation. This means the action of presenting was in progress. Therefore, past continuous tense (plural subject + were + V<sub>ing</sub>) will be used. Hence, ‘were presenting their findings at the conference’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.46.(b) by himself

The phrase ‘by + oneself’ means alone or without company. ‘Himself’ is the correct reflexive pronoun for ‘he’. Hence, ‘by himself’ is the most appropriate answer.
