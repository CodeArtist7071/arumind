## Solutions :-

## Sol.502.(c) as beautiful as

‘As...as’ is a comparative structure used to compare two things. Similarly, the given sentence compares her beauty with a peacock. Hence, ‘as beautiful as’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.503.(b) have

The verb ‘reminds’ indicates an action in present tense. We generally use the semi-modal ‘have to’ with “I/you/we/they” to express a general obligation. Similarly, the given sentence expresses the obligation that I have to be careful.Hence, ‘have’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.504.(b) Career

‘Career’ means the period of time that you spend in your life working or doing a particular thing. The given sentence states that Kiran had a short career as a writer. Hence, ‘career’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.505.(b) pale

‘Pale’ means having a very light skin colour due to sickness. The given sentence states that the patient had a light skin colour and looked tired.. Hence, ‘pale’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.506.(d) Runaway

“Runaway" means one who has escaped from custody. The given sentence states that the police were on high alert because of an escaped convict who had killed many people. Hence, ‘runaway’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.507.(c) cheque

‘Cheque’ means a piece of paper issued by a bank to pay a specific sum of money to a person or entity. The given sentence states that payments can be made by cheque or in cash. Hence, ‘cheque’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.508.(d) heir

‘Heir’ is a person who inherits property or title from a deceased person. The given sentence states that the person is the heir to the throne and should behave accordingly. Hence, ‘heir’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.509.(a) Flock

‘Herd’ is a number of cattle living or feeding together. Similarly, ‘flock’ is a group of birds. Hence, ‘flock’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.510.(d) loud

‘Loud’ means (sound) at a high volume. The given sentence states that they are playing songs so loud that the entire neighbourhood is disturbed. Hence, ‘loud’ is the most appropriate answer.

## Sol.511.(d) aisle

‘Aisle’ means a narrow space between rows of seats or shelves. The given sentence states that there are rows of chairs on both sides of the aisle. Hence, ‘aisle’ is the most appropriate answer.

‘Incessant’ means continuing without pause. The given sentence states that the match schedule was disturbed due to incessant rains. Hence, ‘incessant’ is the most appropriate answer.

The idiom ‘take the wind out of my sails means to cause someone to lose confidence.

The given sentence states that you are told not to make me feel less confident for not making any progress in life. Hence, ‘sails’ is the most appropriate answer.
